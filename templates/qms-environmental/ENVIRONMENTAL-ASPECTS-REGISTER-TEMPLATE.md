# EAR-XXX: Environmental Aspects and Impacts Register

Per ISO 14001:2015 §6.1.2. The Environmental Aspects Register identifies the environmental aspects of the organization's activities, products, and services that it can control + influence; determines associated environmental impacts; and identifies those aspects + impacts that have or can have a significant impact on the environment ("significant environmental aspects" — SEAs). SEAs drive operational controls (§8.1), monitoring + measurement (§9.1.1), emergency preparedness (§8.2), and improvement actions (§6.1.4).

- **Activities + products + services covered:** [enumerated]
- **Lifecycle perspective:** ISO 14001:2015 introduced explicit lifecycle perspective consideration — aspects identified shall consider, where applicable, the value chain from raw material acquisition / generation through use + end-of-life treatment (per §6.1.2).
- **Conditions considered:** normal operating conditions, abnormal conditions, foreseeable emergency situations (§6.1.2 a).

Typical aspect categories to consider:
- **Emissions to air:** VOC, NOx, SOx, PM, CO2-e, refrigerants
- **Discharges to water:** wastewater (process, sanitary, stormwater), spills, runoff
- **Discharges to land:** spills, soil contamination, leachate
- **Use of raw materials + natural resources:** water, virgin material, recycled material, energy (cross-reference to EnMS if ISO 50001 also in scope)
- **Use of energy:** electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, propane, district steam
- **Energy emitted (heat, vibration, radiation, noise, light):** thermal pollution, vibration to neighboring sites, light pollution
- **Generation of waste + by-products:** hazardous waste, non-hazardous waste, recyclables, food waste
- **Physical attributes:** size, shape, color, appearance — relevant where impact is e.g., visual nuisance or biodiversity disruption

## 7. Review + update cadence

Per ISO 14001:2015 §6.1.1 — environmental aspects + their impacts shall be determined considering changes (including planned new developments, new or modified activities, products, services). Triggers for re-review:

- Annually as a minimum
- Site / process / product change
- New / amended regulatory requirement
- New material interested-party expectation
- After any environmental incident
- After internal audit finding affecting this register
- Management review action
