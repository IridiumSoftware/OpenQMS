# PMA-XXX: [Product Name] Premarket Approval Submission Package

Per 21 CFR 814 Subpart B. The PMA submission is the FDA's regulatory submission for Class III medical devices — the most stringent FDA pathway. PMA requires demonstration of safety AND effectiveness through valid scientific evidence (typically including pivotal clinical investigations conducted under an Investigational Device Exemption, IDE).

This template is the **assembly index** for the PMA submission package. Each section references the controlled documents that constitute the actual submission content; the PMA-XXX record itself is the structured manifest that FDA eSubmitter ingests.
