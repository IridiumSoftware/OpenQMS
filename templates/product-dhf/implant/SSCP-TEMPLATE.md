# SSCP-XXX: [Product Name] Summary of Safety and Clinical Performance

Per EU MDR 2017/745 Article 32 and MDCG 2019-9 rev 1 (SSCP template). The SSCP is **mandatory for Class III devices and implantable devices** (other than custom-made or investigational). It is written separately for the **patient** and the **healthcare professional**, validated by the notified body, and published on EUDAMED. It must be updated throughout the device lifecycle as new safety / clinical performance data emerges.

## 3. Update cadence

Per Article 32(3), the SSCP shall be updated at least annually for Class III and implantable devices. Updates are validated by the NB. The current SSCP version on EUDAMED is always the most recent NB-validated version.
