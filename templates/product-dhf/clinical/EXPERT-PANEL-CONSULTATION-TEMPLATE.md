# EPC-XXX: [Product Name] Expert Panel Consultation Record

Per EU MDR 2017/745 Article 54 (Clinical evaluation consultation procedure). Required for:

- **Implantable Class III devices**, AND
- **Class IIb active devices intended to administer or remove a medicinal product**

The Notified Body sends the Clinical Evaluation Assessment Report (CEAR) to the European Commission Expert Panel before issuing its certificate. The expert panel issues a scientific opinion within 60 days; the NB takes this opinion into account in its certification decision.
