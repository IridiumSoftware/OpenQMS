# PMCF-XXX: [Product Name] Post-Market Clinical Follow-up Plan

**Justification for no PMCF** is permitted per Annex XIV Part B §1, but must itself be documented in the CER. Most non-trivial devices, and all implantables / Class III, require PMCF.
