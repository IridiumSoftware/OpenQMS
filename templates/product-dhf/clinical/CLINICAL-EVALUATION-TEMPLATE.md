# CER-XXX: [Product Name] Clinical Evaluation Report

Per EU MDR 2017/745 Annex XIV Part A and MDCG 2020-13 (CER assessment) / MDCG 2020-6 (sufficient clinical evidence for legacy devices) / MDCG 2020-5 (clinical evaluation equivalence). The Clinical Evaluation Report (CER) assesses the clinical safety and performance of the device throughout its lifecycle.

**Per-class customization.** Class I non-measuring devices may rely heavily on equivalence or literature; Class III and implantables typically require clinical investigations (Annex XV). Adopters tailor the depth of each section per device classification and clinical-data availability.
