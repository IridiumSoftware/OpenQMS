# APQR-XXX: Annual Product Quality Review

Per 21 CFR 211.180(e) (US — "Annual Product Quality Review") + EudraLex Vol. 4 Part I Chapter 1 §1.10 (EU — "Product Quality Review" / PQR). The APQR is the periodic (annual) per-product review aggregating manufacturing + quality data over the reporting period to verify the consistency of the existing process + the appropriateness of current specifications for both starting materials + finished product, to highlight any trends + to identify product + process improvements.

PQR is mandatory annually in EU per Ch. 1 §1.10. APQR is required at least annually per 21 CFR 211.180(e). Output must be reviewed by management; trends must be acted upon; the QA-approved report shall be retained per the site's records-retention SOP (typically batch-record retention + 1 year minimum).
