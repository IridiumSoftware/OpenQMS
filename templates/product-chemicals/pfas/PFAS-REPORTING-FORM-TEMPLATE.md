# TSCA PFAS Reporting Form (40 CFR Part 705)

**Template for:** US EPA TSCA Section 8(a)(7) PFAS Reporting and Recordkeeping (40 CFR Part 705, final rule October 2023). Per-substance per-year per-site data structure plus CBI / non-CBI dual-version organisation plus article-importer due-diligence record.
**Submission:** Via EPA Central Data Exchange (CDX) using Chemical Information Submission System (CISS).
**Lookback period:** Calendar years 2011-2022 (11-year window).
**Reporting window:** 2025-07-11 to 2026-01-11 for standard reporters. Small manufacturers reporting solely for article-PFAS: extended to 2026-07-11. **Verify current deadline at submission time — EPA has extended once and may extend further.**
**Retention:** 5 years from last day of submission period (§705.25).

## 6. Article-importer due-diligence record (REQUIRED if importing PFAS in articles)

Per preamble "knowability" standard. Document good-faith due diligence to support both reporting decisions AND defensible non-reporting determinations.

| Article category | Supplier | Country of origin | Years imported | PFAS content known? | Source of knowledge / due diligence performed | Reporting decision (Yes/No + rationale) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | ☐ Confirmed ☐ Suspected ☐ Ruled-out | SDS review / specification review / supplier certification / EU REACH SVHC notification check / Cal Prop 65 / Stockholm Convention POPs / industry guidance / lab test | |

**Common article categories with knowable PFAS content (illustrative, not exhaustive):**
- Fluoropolymer-coated textiles + apparel + carpet (PFOA / PFOS / 6:2 fluorotelomer)
- Greaseproof food packaging + microwave popcorn bags + fast-food wraps
- Non-stick cookware coatings (PTFE)
- Fluoropolymer-lined valves + gaskets + hoses + pump diaphragms (PTFE / FKM)
- Semiconductor manufacturing equipment + photoresists + etchants
- Firefighting foam (AFFF, especially pre-2018 stockpiles)
- Chrome-plating bath mist suppressants (PFOS historical)
- Electronics — printed circuit board fluorinated solder masks; refrigerants + heat-transfer fluids
- Medical devices with fluoropolymer components (catheters; implant coatings)
- Cosmetics + personal care with fluorinated ingredients
- Ski wax + outdoor gear water-repellent treatments
- Automotive — fuel hoses + brake lines + EV thermal management; refrigerants
