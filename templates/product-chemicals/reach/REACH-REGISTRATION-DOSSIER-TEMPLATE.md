# REACH-DOSSIER-XXX: REACH Registration Dossier Outline for [Substance Name]

Per **EU REACH Regulation (EC) 1907/2006** Articles 5-6 (No data, no market) + Article 10 (Information required for registration) + Annex VI (Information requirements per Article 10) + Annexes VII-X (technical dossier per tonnage band) + Annex I (Chemical Safety Report — required for substances ≥10 tonnes/year).

REACH is the cornerstone EU chemical regulation. **Substances ≥1 tonne/year per manufacturer/importer require registration with ECHA via REACH-IT before being placed on the EU market.** Joint submission via SIEF (Substance Information Exchange Forum) is required for the same substance from multiple registrants.

## 5. Technical dossier (Annex VII through Annex X — tonnage-dependent)

Information requirements scale with tonnage band:

| Tonnage band | Annex | Information required |
|---|---|---|
| ≥1 to <10 t/y | Annex VII | Physico-chemical + toxicological + ecotoxicological (limited; in vitro where possible per Article 25) |
| ≥10 to <100 t/y | Annex VIII | Adds in vivo acute studies + sub-acute toxicity |
| ≥100 to <1000 t/y | Annex IX | Adds 90-day repeat-dose + reproductive toxicity screening + chronic ecotox |
| ≥1000 t/y | Annex X | Adds carcinogenicity + reproductive toxicity (multi-generation) + chronic toxicity (12+ month) |

### 5.1 Physico-chemical properties (Annex VII Section 7)

State + melting point + boiling point + density + vapor pressure + surface tension + water solubility + partition coefficient n-octanol/water (log Kow) + flash point + flammability + explosive properties + oxidising properties + auto-ignition + granulometry (solids) + stability in organic solvents.

### 5.2 Toxicological information (Annex VII §8 + scales with tonnage)

Skin irritation/corrosion; eye irritation; skin sensitisation; mutagenicity (in vitro + in vivo); acute toxicity (oral + inhalation + dermal); repeat-dose toxicity (sub-acute / sub-chronic / chronic per tonnage); reproductive toxicity; carcinogenicity (≥1000 t/y); toxicokinetics.

### 5.3 Ecotoxicological information (Annex VII §9 + scales with tonnage)

Aquatic toxicity (fish + invertebrates + algae — acute + chronic per tonnage); biodegradation (ready + inherent + simulation); abiotic degradation (hydrolysis + photolysis); adsorption/desorption; PBT + vPvB assessment per Annex XIII; sediment toxicity; terrestrial toxicity (soil organisms + plants); secondary poisoning.

### 5.4 Read-across + grouping (Annex XI §1.5)

Where applicable: structural-analog read-across + category approach + (Q)SAR results. Read-across justification per ECHA RAAF (Read-Across Assessment Framework).

## 6. Chemical Safety Report (Annex I) — required for substances ≥10 tonnes/year per Article 14

### 6.1 Human health hazard assessment

- DNEL (Derived No-Effect Level) per relevant population + exposure route + time
- DMEL where DNEL not derivable

### 6.2 Environmental hazard assessment

- PNEC (Predicted No-Effect Concentration) per environmental compartment

### 6.3 PBT + vPvB assessment

Per Annex XIII criteria. SVHC implications if positive.

### 6.4 Exposure assessment (when classified as hazardous OR PBT/vPvB)

- Exposure scenarios per identified use
- Use descriptor codes
- Exposure estimation (ECETOC TRA + EUSES + measured data)

### 6.5 Risk characterisation

- RCR (Risk Characterisation Ratio) = exposure / DNEL (human) or PEC/PNEC (environmental); RCR <1 = controlled risk

## 7. SVHC + Authorisation (Title VII) considerations

If the substance is listed on:
- **Candidate List (Article 59)** — Article 33 communication obligation (≥0.1% w/w in articles); Article 7(2) notification if ≥1 tonne/year in articles
- **Annex XIV Authorisation List** — sunset date + application for authorisation per Article 60 required
- **Annex XVII Restrictions** — restriction conditions must be observed

## 8. Submission to ECHA via REACH-IT

- IUCLID dossier prepared per Annex VI structure
- REACH-IT submission portal
- Fee payment per ECHA fees regulation (varies by tonnage band + size of registrant)
- Business confidentiality claims per Article 119
- Technical completeness check (TCC) before processing
- Substance evaluation (CoRAP) may follow

## 9. Post-submission obligations

- **Update obligations per Article 22:** new information; changes in tonnage band; new uses; updated CSR; CLP classification change
- **Annual update of tonnage banded** if exceeds threshold
- **Dossier update fees**
