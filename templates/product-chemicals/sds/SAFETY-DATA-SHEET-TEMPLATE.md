# SDS-XXX: Safety Data Sheet for [Substance/Mixture Name]

Per **UN GHS Rev. 10** + **EU CLP Regulation (EC) 1272/2008** + **EU REACH Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 Annex II** (REACH dictates SDS *format*; CLP dictates classification driving SDS content) + **US OSHA HCS 29 CFR 1910.1200 Appendix D** (US 16-section format aligned with GHS).

The Safety Data Sheet is the foundational risk-communication artifact for chemicals. **16-section format mandatory globally** (GHS-harmonized; minor regional content variations).

- **Product identifier:** [trade name + REACH registration number(s) for substances + CAS + EC + UFI for mixtures (per CLP Annex VIII)]
- **Other means of identification:** [synonyms, REACH number, IUPAC name]
- **Recommended use:** [identified uses + uses advised against per REACH Article 14]
- **Supplier details:** [legal name + address + email + phone — also REACH-Only Representative (OR) for non-EU manufacturers]
- **Emergency phone number:** [24/7; specify country coverage]

## Section 2 — Hazard(s) identification

- **GHS classification:** [per all hazard classes — physical / health / environmental — assigned per CLP Article 4 (EU) or OSHA HCS App A (US)]
- **GHS label elements:** signal word + hazard pictogram(s) + H-statement(s) + P-statement(s)
- **Other hazards not classified:** PBT/vPvB per REACH Annex XIII; endocrine disruptor properties

## Section 3 — Composition / information on ingredients

For substances: identity + impurities + stabilisers ≥1% (or ≥0.1% for CMR / PBT / vPvB).
For mixtures: identity of components ≥1% (≥0.1% for CMR/PBT/vPvB + SVHC).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Section 4 — First-aid measures

- Inhalation / Skin contact / Eye contact / Ingestion: per route specific first aid
- Most important symptoms + effects (acute + delayed)
- Indication of immediate medical attention + special treatment needed

## Section 5 — Fire-fighting measures

- Suitable / unsuitable extinguishing media
- Special hazards (decomposition products + combustion gases)
- Advice for fire-fighters (PPE + special procedures)

## Section 6 — Accidental release measures

- Personal precautions + PPE + emergency procedures
- Environmental precautions (containment + prevention of release to soil/water)
- Methods + materials for containment + clean-up

## Section 7 — Handling + storage

- Precautions for safe handling (engineering controls + PPE + worker training)
- Conditions for safe storage (incompatibilities + container materials + temperature/humidity)
- Specific end use(s) [per REACH exposure scenarios where applicable]

## Section 8 — Exposure controls + personal protection

- **Control parameters:** OEL (Occupational Exposure Limits) — WEL / TLV-TWA / MAK / DNEL / PNEC (REACH-derived no-effect levels)
- **Engineering controls:** local exhaust + general ventilation
- **PPE:** respiratory + hand + eye + skin
- **Environmental exposure controls:** containment

## Section 9 — Physical + chemical properties

Appearance + odor + odor threshold + pH + melting point + boiling point + flash point + evaporation rate + flammability + UFL/LFL + vapor pressure + vapor density + relative density + solubility + partition coefficient + auto-ignition + decomposition + viscosity + explosive properties + oxidising properties.

## Section 10 — Stability + reactivity

- Reactivity / chemical stability / hazardous reactions
- Conditions to avoid + incompatible materials + hazardous decomposition products

## Section 11 — Toxicological information

Per route (oral + dermal + inhalation): LD50 + LC50; skin/eye irritation + corrosion; respiratory/skin sensitisation; germ-cell mutagenicity; carcinogenicity (IARC + NTP + OSHA + ACGIH + EU CLH); reproductive toxicity; STOT (single + repeated exposure); aspiration hazard.

## Section 12 — Ecological information

Acute + chronic aquatic toxicity (fish + invertebrates + algae); persistence + degradability; bioaccumulative potential (Log Kow + BCF); mobility in soil; PBT + vPvB assessment per REACH Annex XIII; other adverse effects.

## Section 13 — Disposal considerations

Waste treatment methods; waste codes (EU Waste Catalogue + EPA RCRA codes); contaminated packaging.

## Section 14 — Transport information

UN number + UN proper shipping name + transport hazard class(es) + packing group; environmental hazards; special precautions for user.
- **DOT** (US — 49 CFR 100-185)
- **IMDG** (sea — IMO)
- **IATA** (air)
- **ADR/RID** (EU road + rail)

## Section 15 — Regulatory information

- Safety, health, environmental regulations specific to the product
- **EU:** REACH registration status; SVHC candidate list (Article 59); Authorisation list (Annex XIV); Restriction list (Annex XVII); CLP harmonised classification (Annex VI); seveso scope (Directive 2012/18/EU); ozone-depleting substances (Reg. EC 1005/2009); POPs (Reg. EU 2019/1021)
- **US:** TSCA inventory status + Section 5 PMN; SARA Title III §302/304/311/312/313; CERCLA RQ; California Prop 65; Massachusetts Right-to-Know
- **Chemical Safety Assessment per REACH** (≥10 tonnes/year — annex to SDS)

## Section 16 — Other information

- Abbreviations + acronyms
- Date of preparation + revision history with changes summary
- References to source data
- Disclaimer (data correct to best knowledge; user responsible for safe-use determination)

## Maintenance triggers (per REACH Article 31(9))

The SDS shall be promptly updated:
- New information that may affect risk-management measures
- New information on hazards becomes available
- Authorisation has been granted or refused
- Restriction has been imposed
- Any change that requires updating exposure scenarios

Recipients receive the updated version free of charge (paper + electronic) for any substance/mixture supplied in the preceding 12 months.
