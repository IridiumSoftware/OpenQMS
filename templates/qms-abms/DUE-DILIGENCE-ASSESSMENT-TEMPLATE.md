# DDA-XXX: Anti-Bribery Due Diligence Assessment

Per ISO 37001:2016 §8.2 (Due diligence) + §8.3 (Financial controls) + §8.4 (Non-financial controls) + §8.5 (Anti-bribery controls of controlled organizations and business associates). Risk-proportionate evaluation of a third party prior to engagement and periodically thereafter. The depth + rigor of due diligence scales with the assessed bribery risk associated with the relationship.

Required for any business associate (supplier, agent, distributor, consultant, joint-venture partner, intermediary, broker) where the risk assessment indicates more than low bribery risk — particularly where the third party will interact with public officials, operate in high-risk jurisdictions, or have significant decision-making authority on behalf of the organization.

## 5. Anti-bribery commitment evidence

For Medium + High tiers — third party shall provide:

- [ ] Signed anti-bribery commitment / certification
- [ ] Copy of their own anti-bribery / anti-corruption policy
- [ ] Evidence of anti-bribery training program for their personnel
- [ ] Confirmation of no pending bribery / corruption investigations or convictions
- [ ] Beneficial-ownership disclosure
- [ ] Disclosure of PEP relationships
- [ ] Disclosure of public-official affiliations

## 6. Contractual safeguards required

The agreement with this third party shall include (per ISO 37001 §8.5 + organization's ABMS contractual template):

- [ ] Anti-bribery / anti-corruption clause with right to terminate on breach
- [ ] Compliance-with-laws clause (FCPA, UK Bribery Act, local anti-corruption laws of relevant jurisdictions)
- [ ] Audit rights — the organization may audit third party's relevant records
- [ ] Training requirement — third-party personnel touching relevant activities undergo anti-bribery training
- [ ] Sub-contracting restrictions or flow-down obligations
- [ ] Reporting / disclosure obligation — third party reports any suspected bribery, investigation, or enforcement contact
- [ ] No-facilitation-payments clause (where organization policy prohibits them)
- [ ] Termination on adverse change (sanctions listing, criminal indictment, material change in ownership)
