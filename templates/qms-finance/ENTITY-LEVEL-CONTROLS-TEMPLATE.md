# FIN-ELC-001: Entity-Level Controls Assessment

Documents and evaluates entity-level controls across the COSO 2013 **5 components / 17 principles**. The precision of these controls informs how much process-level testing is required.

## 1. Control Environment (Principles 1–5)
[Integrity & ethical values (code of conduct) · board/audit-committee independence + oversight · structures, authorities, reporting lines · commitment to competence · accountability (incentives, evaluations).]

## 2. Risk Assessment (Principles 6–9)
[Financial-reporting objectives clarity · risk identification/analysis · **fraud risk incl. management override** · significant-change identification.]

## 3. Control Activities (Principles 10–12)
[Selection of control activities · general controls over technology (→ ITGC) · deployment via policies + procedures.]

## 4. Information & Communication (Principles 13–15)
[Relevant quality information · internal communication of control responsibilities · external communication.]

## 5. Monitoring Activities (Principles 16–17)
[Ongoing + separate evaluations (internal audit) · timely evaluation + communication of deficiencies to the audit committee.]

## 6. Conclusion
[Per-principle present-and-functioning conclusion + overall entity-level effectiveness; cross-reference the [ICFR Control Matrix](ICFR-CONTROL-MATRIX-TEMPLATE.md).]
