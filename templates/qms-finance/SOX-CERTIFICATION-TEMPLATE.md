# FIN-CERT-[PERIOD]-001: SOX Certification

The principal executive and financial officers' certifications. **§302** is filed each periodic report (10-Q/10-K); the **§404(a)** management ICFR assessment accompanies the annual report (10-K). Reflects SEC Exchange Act Rules 13a-15 / 15d-15.

## 1. §302 — disclosure-controls certification (each period)

[CEO + CFO certify, for the period: the report does not contain material misstatements/omissions and fairly presents financial condition; they are responsible for DC&P and ICFR; they have evaluated DC&P effectiveness as of period end; and they have disclosed to the auditor and audit committee any significant deficiencies, material weaknesses, and any fraud involving management. Note any **material change to ICFR** in the period.]

## 2. §404(a) — annual management ICFR assessment (10-K)

- **Framework:** COSO Internal Control — Integrated Framework (2013).
- **Basis:** the [Control Matrix](ICFR-CONTROL-MATRIX-TEMPLATE.md) + [Entity-Level Controls](ENTITY-LEVEL-CONTROLS-TEMPLATE.md) + [Test Results](CONTROL-TEST-PLAN-AND-RESULTS-TEMPLATE.md) + the [Deficiency Log](CONTROL-DEFICIENCY-LOG-TEMPLATE.md).
- **Conclusion:** [ICFR is / is not effective as of period end]; disclose any **material weakness**.
- **§404(b):** [auditor attestation status, for accelerated/large-accelerated filers].
