# FIN-TEST-[PERIOD]-001: Control Test Plan and Results

Evidence of **design effectiveness** and **operating effectiveness** for the key controls in the [Control Matrix](ICFR-CONTROL-MATRIX-TEMPLATE.md) (PCAOB AS 2201). Organized to support the §404(a) management assessment and the §404(b) auditor attestation.

## 1. Approach

- **Design effectiveness:** walkthroughs confirming the control, as designed, would prevent or detect a material misstatement.
- **Operating effectiveness:** test of operation via inquiry + observation + inspection + re-performance.
- **Sample sizes** by control frequency (e.g., annual 1 · quarterly 2 · monthly 2–5 · weekly 5–15 · daily 20–40 · recurring/automated 1 + ITGC reliance). Interim results **rolled forward** to period end.
