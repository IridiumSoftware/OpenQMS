# ENO-XXX: Energy Objectives and Targets Register

Per ISO 50001:2018 §6.2 — "The organization shall establish energy objectives at relevant functions, levels, processes or facilities within the organization. The energy objectives shall: be consistent with the energy policy; be measurable (if practicable); take into account applicable legal requirements and other requirements; consider its SEUs; consider opportunities to improve energy performance; be monitored; be communicated; be updated as appropriate."

This register is the dedicated documented information for §6.2. Informed by the Energy Review + EnPIs + EnB (ER-XXX per §6.3 + §6.4 + §6.5) — objectives target improvements vs. the EnB measured by the EnPI(s).

## 7. Update triggers

- Material change in operations affecting energy use
- New SEU identified or SEU retired

- Significant deviation from expected energy consumption per §9.1.1
- Annual review per cadence
- New legal requirement or voluntary commitment
- Strategic-priority change
