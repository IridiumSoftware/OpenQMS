## 8. Data subject + consumer rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

**GDPR Rights (data subjects in EEA + UK + Switzerland):**
- **Access** (Article 15) — request a copy of your personal data + processing information
- **Rectification** (Article 16) — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten", Article 17) — delete your data subject to 5 exceptions
- **Restriction** (Article 18) — limit processing in 4 enumerated circumstances
- **Portability** (Article 20) — receive your data in structured, commonly-used, machine-readable format + transmit to another controller
- **Object** (Article 21) — to processing based on legitimate interests; absolute right for direct marketing
- **Automated decision-making** (Article 22) — not be subject to solely-automated decisions with legal/significant effect; obtain human intervention
- **Withdraw consent** (Article 7(3)) — where processing is based on consent; as easy to withdraw as to give
- **Lodge complaint** with supervisory authority (Article 77)

**CCPA/CPRA Rights (California consumers):**
- **Right to know** what personal information is collected, used, shared, sold (§1798.100/110/115)
- **Right to delete** personal information (§1798.105)
- **Right to correct** inaccurate personal information (§1798.106)
- **Right to opt-out of sale + sharing** for cross-context behavioral advertising (§1798.120 + 135)
- **Right to limit use of SPI** to that necessary for services reasonably expected (§1798.121)
- **Right to non-discrimination** for exercising rights (§1798.125)
- **Right to data portability** (§1798.100(d))
- **Right against retaliation** for exercising labor-related privacy rights
