# Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

**Template for:** GDPR Article 35 Data Protection Impact Assessment + CCPA Risk Assessment per CPPA regulations §7150-7157 (2025-2026 final).
**Trigger:** Processing likely to result in HIGH RISK to rights + freedoms of natural persons. Article 35(3) automatic triggers + each supervisory authority's positive list. CCPA Risk Assessment trigger thresholds per CPPA regs §7150.
**Outcome:** Decision to proceed (with mitigations) / proceed with prior consultation per Article 36 / not proceed. Reviewed at least annually + on material change.
