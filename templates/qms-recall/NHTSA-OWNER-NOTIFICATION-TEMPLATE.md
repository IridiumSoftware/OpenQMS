# NHTSA-OWN-XXX: Owner Notification Letter (49 CFR Part 577)

Per 49 CFR Part 577 — Defect and Noncompliance Notification. This is the letter mailed to each first purchaser (and subsequent owners when reasonably ascertainable) of vehicles or equipment subject to a recall under 49 CFR 573.

**Timing requirement (§577.7):** First notification no later than 60 days after submission of the Defect Information Report (DIR) to NHTSA per 49 CFR 573.6, unless NHTSA grants an extension. Second notification per §577.8 required if remedy completion rate < 70% at 6 months from first notification.

**Content requirements (§577.5):** Letter MUST contain each of the specific elements listed in §1 through §10 below in vehicle-owner-comprehensible language. NHTSA reviews + may require revisions before mailing.

**Format requirements (§577.6):** First-class mail to last-known owner address (state-DMV records or manufacturer records, whichever more current). For motorcycles, equipment, child restraint systems, replacement equipment — adapted addressing per §577.6(c).

## §577.8 — Second notification trigger

If at 6 months from first notification the completion rate (remedies performed ÷ vehicles in scope) is < 70%, [Manufacturer Name] shall send a second notification to owners whose vehicles have not yet been remedied. The second notification:

- Cites the same recall campaign number
- May use modified content emphasizing urgency
- May be sent by different means (postcard, email if owner consents, etc.) per NHTSA approval
- Documented in this REC-XXX record

## §577.9 — Reimbursement (where pre-notification remedies occurred)

If owners were charged for pre-notification remedies of the same defect/noncompliance, the manufacturer shall reimburse those costs per §577.9. Reimbursement program described in a separate notice if applicable.
