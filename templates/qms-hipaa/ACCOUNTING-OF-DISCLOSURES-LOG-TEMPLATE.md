# HIPAA Accounting of Disclosures Log

**Template for:** HIPAA Privacy Rule §164.528 accounting of disclosures. Maintained as an ongoing log of accountable disclosures of PHI; on individual request, CE produces an accounting covering the prior 6 years (or shorter period if requested).
**Response window:** 60 days from request; one 30-day extension permitted with written notification per §164.528(c)(1).
**Fees:** First accounting in any 12-month period FREE; reasonable cost-based fee permitted for subsequent within same 12-month period with individual notification of fee + opportunity to withdraw or modify request per §164.528(c)(2).
**Retention:** 6 years per §164.316 + §164.530(j).

---

## 1. Disclosures EXEMPT from §164.528 accounting

The following disclosures DO NOT require accounting per §164.528(a)(1) — DO NOT log here (logged separately if needed for other purposes):

- (i) Disclosures for **Treatment, Payment, Healthcare Operations** (TPO) per §164.506
- (ii) Disclosures **to the individual** of their own PHI
- (iii) Disclosures pursuant to written **Authorization** per §164.508
- (iv) Disclosures for **facility directory** per §164.510(a)
- (v) Disclosures to **persons involved in individual's care** per §164.510(b) (family, friends, others identified by individual)
- (vi) Disclosures for **national security or intelligence** purposes per §164.512(k)(2)
- (vii) Disclosures to **correctional institutions or law enforcement officials** per §164.512(k)(5)
- (viii) Disclosures that occurred **before April 14, 2003** (Privacy Rule compliance date)
- (ix) Limited Data Set disclosures per §164.514(e) (with Data Use Agreement; not subject to accounting)

## 4. Temporary suspension (§164.528(a)(2))

Health-oversight agency OR law-enforcement official may temporarily suspend an individual's right to receive an accounting of certain disclosures by providing CE a written statement that accounting would impede the agency's activities. Suspension period as specified by agency.
