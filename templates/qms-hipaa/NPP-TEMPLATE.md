# Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP)

**Template for:** HIPAA Privacy Rule §164.520 Notice of Privacy Practices.
**Scope:** Required by every Covered Entity that maintains PHI. Distributed: to individuals at first delivery of service (direct treatment providers) OR with enrollment materials (health plans); posted at provider service-delivery locations + on website if maintained.
**Revisions:** When material change to legal duties or privacy practices; effective date of revision noted. Health plans must notify enrollees of material revisions within 60 days.

## 3. Uses + disclosures REQUIRING your written Authorization

We will obtain your written Authorization per §164.508 before:
- Sale of PHI (HITECH-prohibited absent Authorization)
- Marketing communications (with limited TPO exceptions)
- Psychotherapy notes (specific Authorization required)
- Most research uses (unless IRB waiver applies)
- Other uses + disclosures not described in §2

You may revoke an Authorization at any time in writing, except to the extent we have already acted in reliance on it.

## 4. Your individual rights

### 4.1 Right of access (§164.524)

You have the right to inspect + obtain a copy of your PHI in our Designated Record Set. Request in writing using [process / form]. We will respond within **30 days** of receipt (one 30-day extension allowed with notice). Fees limited to labor for copying + supplies + postage + electronic media if requested in electronic form.

### 4.2 Right of amendment (§164.526)

You have the right to request amendment of inaccurate or incomplete PHI in your Designated Record Set. Request in writing using [process / form]. We will respond within **60 days** (one 30-day extension allowed). If we deny, we will provide written explanation; you may submit a Statement of Disagreement which we will include in the record.

### 4.3 Right to an Accounting of Disclosures (§164.528)

You have the right to receive an accounting of disclosures made of your PHI for the **prior 6 years** (or shorter period if requested), with limited exceptions (TPO disclosures, Authorization-based, facility directory, to you, incidental). First accounting in any 12-month period is free; reasonable cost-based fee for subsequent.

### 4.4 Right to request restrictions (§164.522(a))

You may request restrictions on uses/disclosures of your PHI for TPO. We are not required to agree (except: HITECH-mandated restriction on disclosures to health plan when you pay out-of-pocket in full for the service).

### 4.5 Right to request confidential communications (§164.522(b))

You may request that we communicate with you about your PHI by alternative means (e.g., phone + not mail) or at alternative locations. We will accommodate reasonable requests.

### 4.6 Right to a paper copy of this Notice

Even if you have agreed to receive this Notice electronically, you have the right to obtain a paper copy from us upon request.

### 4.7 Right to file a complaint

You may file a complaint with us per §10 below OR directly with the HHS Office for Civil Rights:
- https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/filing-a-complaint/
- 200 Independence Avenue, S.W., Washington, D.C. 20201
- 1-877-696-6775

**You will NOT be retaliated against** for filing a complaint per §164.530(g).

### 4.8 Right to be notified of a breach (§164.404)

You have the right to be notified of breach of your unsecured PHI within **60 days** of discovery.

## 5. Reproductive Health Care Privacy (effective December 23, 2024)

Per the HHS Final Rule strengthening HIPAA Privacy Rule protections for Reproductive Health Care (89 FR 32976, April 2024):

- We will not use or disclose PHI to investigate or impose liability for the seeking, obtaining, providing, or facilitating of lawful reproductive health care
- We will obtain a signed Attestation per §164.509 before disclosing PHI potentially related to reproductive health care for health oversight, judicial/administrative, law enforcement, or coroner/medical-examiner purposes
- Restrictions apply when health care is lawful in the state where provided OR lawful under federal law
