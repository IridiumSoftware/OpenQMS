# HIPAA Authorization for Use or Disclosure of PHI

**Template for:** HIPAA Privacy Rule §164.508 written Authorization. Required when a CE wishes to use or disclose PHI for purposes NOT permitted by Privacy Rule (TPO + 12 enumerated §164.512 exceptions + permitted-without-authorisation cases). All other uses/disclosures require Authorization.
**Use cases:** marketing communications (with limited TPO exceptions) · sale of PHI (HITECH-prohibited absent Authorization) · psychotherapy notes · most research uses · disclosure to employers · life-insurance underwriting · custom data requests beyond TPO scope.
**Compound authorisations** generally prohibited per §164.508(b)(3); research authorisations may be combined with consent to participate in research.

## 2. Required statements (§164.508(c)(2))

The following statements MUST appear in the Authorization (typically grouped at the bottom):

### 2.1 Right to revoke (§164.508(c)(2)(i))

> You have the right to revoke this Authorization at any time by sending a written revocation to [contact at §6.1 below]. Revocation will be effective upon our receipt, except to the extent that we have already taken action in reliance on this Authorization. If this Authorization was obtained as a condition of obtaining insurance coverage, other law may give the insurer the right to contest a claim under the policy.

### 2.2 Treatment, payment, enrollment, eligibility conditioning prohibition (§164.508(c)(2)(ii))

> [CE] will NOT condition treatment, payment, enrollment in a health plan, or eligibility for benefits on whether you sign this Authorization, EXCEPT: (i) for research that involves treatment when CE may condition research-related treatment on Authorization; (ii) when health plan obtains Authorization for underwriting / eligibility determinations (limited; cannot use genetic information); (iii) when Authorization is obtained for healthcare solely for the purpose of creating PHI for disclosure to a third party.

### 2.3 Potential for re-disclosure (§164.508(c)(2)(iii))

> Information disclosed pursuant to this Authorization may be subject to re-disclosure by the recipient + may no longer be protected by federal privacy regulations. State law may continue to protect certain types of information after re-disclosure.

### 2.4 Marketing / sale of PHI (§164.508(a)(3) + (a)(4))

If Authorization is for marketing communications + remuneration is received from a third party, additional statement: > [CE] will receive financial remuneration from [third party] in exchange for making this disclosure.

If Authorization is for sale of PHI: > This Authorization will result in [CE] receiving direct or indirect remuneration in exchange for your PHI.

## 3. Validity check

| Defect that invalidates Authorization (§164.508(b)(2)) | Status |
|---|---|
| Expiration date passed OR expiration event occurred | ☐ Valid ☐ EXPIRED |
| Authorization not filled out completely with respect to §164.508(c) required elements | ☐ Complete ☐ INCOMPLETE |
| Authorization known to have been revoked | ☐ Active ☐ REVOKED |
| Authorization is COMPOUND prohibited per §164.508(b)(3) | ☐ OK ☐ COMPOUND-PROHIBITED |
| Material information in Authorization known to be false | ☐ OK ☐ FALSE |

If ANY defect identified → Authorization invalid; use/disclosure under it not permitted.
