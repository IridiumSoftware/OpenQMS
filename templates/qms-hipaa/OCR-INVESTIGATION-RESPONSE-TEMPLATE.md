# HHS OCR Compliance Investigation Response

**Template for:** Response to HHS Office for Civil Rights (OCR) compliance investigation. OCR investigates complaints, compliance reviews (proactive), + breach reports (mandatory per §164.408). OCR may impose Civil Monetary Penalties per §160.404 + Corrective Action Plans (CAPs) per Resolution Agreements.
**Posture:** Cooperate fully. Engage legal counsel. Document everything. Respond by stated deadlines. Privileged-vs-disclosable communications carefully segregated.
**Retention:** Investigation records 6 years per §164.316 + §164.530(j); CAP records as specified in Resolution Agreement (often 3-year monitoring period).

### 8.1 Civil Monetary Penalty tier (if not resolved via Resolution Agreement)

Per §160.404 HITECH 4-tier structure (2024 annual inflation adjustments per HHS):

| Tier | Trigger | Per-violation range | Per-year cap on identical violations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unknowing | ~$137 - $68,928 (2024 dollars) | ~$2.067M |
| 2 | Reasonable cause | ~$1,379 - $68,928 | ~$2.067M |
| 3 | Willful neglect corrected within 30 days | ~$13,785 - $68,928 | ~$2.067M |
| 4 | Willful neglect not corrected | ~$68,928 minimum | ~$2.067M |

Criminal penalties per 42 USC §1320d-6 (separate from civil; DOJ-prosecuted): knowing — $50k + 1yr; false pretenses — $100k + 5yr; intent to sell/transfer for personal gain — $250k + 10yr.
