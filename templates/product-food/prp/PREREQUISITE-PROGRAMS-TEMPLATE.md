# PRP-XXX: Prerequisite Programs

Per ISO 22000:2018 §8.2 + ISO/TS 22002-1 (food manufacturing) / 22002-3 (farming) / 22002-4 (food packaging manufacturing) / 22002-5 (transport + storage) + Codex Alimentarius CXC 1-1969 General Principles of Food Hygiene + 21 CFR 117 Subpart B (cGMP).

**Prerequisite Programs (PRPs)** are the foundational hygienic + manufacturing conditions necessary to maintain a hygienic environment throughout the food chain. They are the substrate on which the HACCP plan + Operational PRPs (oPRPs) sit. PRPs are **not specific to a particular hazard** — they manage the operating environment to prevent hazards from being introduced.

This document indexes the PRP suite for the facility. Each PRP element is typically a standalone SOP — this document is the master index + maturity assessment.
