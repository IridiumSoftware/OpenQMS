# HACCP-XXX: HACCP Plan for [Product / Process Family]

Per Codex Alimentarius CXC 1-1969 (General Principles of Food Hygiene — HACCP Annex, revised 2020) + ISO 22000:2018 §8.5 + 21 CFR 117 Subpart C (FSMA Preventive Controls for Human Food). The HACCP plan documents the systematic application of the seven HACCP principles to a specific product or process family: hazard analysis, CCP identification, critical-limit establishment, monitoring, corrective action, verification, and record-keeping.

For organizations operating under FSMA's Preventive Controls Rule (21 CFR 117), the HACCP plan integrates with the Food Safety Plan (which is broader — covering Preventive Controls beyond CCPs: process / allergen / sanitation / supply-chain controls).

## 4. Hazard analysis (Principle 1)

For each process step:

| Step # | Step | Potential hazard (B / C / P / A) | Hazard description | Likelihood | Severity | Significant? (Y/N) | Justification | Control measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Hazard types:**
- **B — Biological:** pathogens (Salmonella, Listeria, E. coli O157:H7, Campylobacter, Clostridium botulinum, Cronobacter sakazakii, Shigella, Vibrio, viruses, parasites)
- **C — Chemical:** mycotoxins, allergens (cross-contact), heavy metals, pesticides, antibiotics, cleaning chemical residues, food additives misuse
- **P — Physical:** metal, glass, hard plastic, stones, wood, bone, jewelry
- **A — Allergens:** Big 9 in US per FALCPA + FASTER Act (milk, egg, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, sesame). EU FIC Annex II 14 allergens.

## 5. Critical Control Points (CCPs) determination (Principle 2)

Apply Codex decision tree (or equivalent) to each significant hazard from §4:

| Hazard # | Step | Q1: Control measure exists? | Q2: Step eliminates / reduces hazard to acceptable level? | Q3: Could contamination occur / increase to unacceptable level? | Q4: Subsequent step eliminates / reduces? | Designation (CCP / oPRP / PRP) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Outputs:**
- **CCP** — step where control is essential AND no subsequent step controls (e.g., thermal kill step for pathogen elimination).
- **oPRP** (Operational PRP per ISO 22000) — control essential, but a CCP-grade decision tree doesn't apply (e.g., allergen segregation, metal detection where rework downstream).
- **PRP** — managed via the foundational prerequisite program (sanitation, pest control, GMPs).

## 8. Corrective actions (Principle 5)

## 11. Linked Food Safety Plan elements (FSMA scope)

Per 21 CFR 117 Subpart C — the Food Safety Plan extends beyond HACCP to include:

- **Process controls** (typically map onto HACCP CCPs)
- **Allergen controls** (cross-contact prevention; label control)
- **Sanitation controls** (zone-based environmental monitoring; cleaning validation)
- **Supply-chain controls** (supplier verification for hazards requiring a supply-chain-applied control)
- **Recall plan** (linked recall procedure)

Each is documented either in this HACCP plan or in the Food Safety Plan (FSP-XXX). Cross-references maintained.
