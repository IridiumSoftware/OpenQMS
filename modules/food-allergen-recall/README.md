# Food Allergen Recall — Open QMS cross-overlay

**Cross-overlay.** Binds food-safety + recall-workflow + privacy to encode the food-allergen-specific recall regulatory framework. Undeclared allergens are the #1 cause of FDA Class I food recalls in the US.

## Scope

Food manufacturers + importers + processors subject to FDA recall regimes when undeclared major allergens detected. FALCPA + FASTER Act add sesame as 9th major allergen effective 2023-01-01.

## Standards covered

7 clauses across FALCPA + FASTER Act 9 major allergens (milk + eggs + fish + crustacean shellfish + tree nuts + peanuts + wheat + soybeans + **sesame**) + 21 CFR 7 recall classification + Reportable Food Registry + 21 CFR 117 FSMA Preventive Controls + privacy-overlay coordination for consumer-notification data handling.

## Composition

Most natural: `food-safety + recall-workflow + privacy + food-allergen-recall`. May add `iso-22000` if FSSC certified. May add `food-fsvp` if importer scope.

## When to use

- Food manufacturers + processors + importers + repackers
- Companies that have experienced past allergen recall (lesson-learned codification)
- Loyalty-program operators with consumer contact data (recall notification triggers privacy considerations)

## Key adopter focus areas

1. **Sesame transition (FASTER Act 2021)** — Industry-wide reformulation OR explicit-declaration adjustment. Verify all sesame-containing OR cross-contact-risk products labeled correctly.
2. **Consumer notification ≥98% target** — Class I requires aggressive recovery; multi-channel (postal + email + phone + retail POS + press + social media + RFR)
3. **Privacy-compliant consumer outreach** — GDPR 6(1)(c) + CCPA §1798.145(a)(1) compliance-with-legal-obligation; data minimisation; NPP/Privacy Policy disclosure
4. **24-hour RFR reporting** — Reportable Food Registry timely submission

## Templates

Reuses existing recall + HACCP + SOP templates with allergen-extension bindings. No new templates.
