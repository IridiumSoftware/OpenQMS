# ISO 37301 General Business profile — Open QMS sub-overlay

Sub-overlay on the `iso-37301` cross-cutting overlay. **Default general-business compliance management profile** for adopters not in public-sector, financial-services, or healthcare.

## Composition

`iso-37301 + iso-37301-general-business`. Often composes with eu-gpsr + recall-workflow + privacy + iso-27001 + iso-37001.

## When to use

- General commercial enterprise outside the public-sector / financial-services / healthcare sectoral profiles
- Multi-line businesses where no single sector dominates
- Default starting profile pending sectoral specialisation
