# ISO 37301 Financial Services profile — Open QMS sub-overlay

Sub-overlay on the `iso-37301` cross-cutting overlay. **Financial-services compliance management profile**.

## Composition

`iso-37301 + iso-37301-financial-services`. Often paired with `dora` (EU) and/or `defense-cui` (NYDFS Part 500 analog) for operational-resilience coordination.

## When to use

- Banks + investment firms + insurance + payments + fintech
- Adopters subject to prudential supervision (Basel III/IV, Solvency II)
- Adopters subject to AML/CFT regimes (FATF, EU AMLD6, BSA, OFAC)
- Adopters subject to consumer-protection regimes (CFPB, FCA SMCR, EU CCD/MCD)
