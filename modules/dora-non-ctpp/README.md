# DORA Non-CTPP — Open QMS sub-overlay

Sub-overlay on the `dora` cross-cutting overlay. **Baseline DORA regime for ICT third-party providers NOT designated CTPP**.

## Scope

Most SaaS vendors / managed-service providers / payment processors serving EU financial entities. Indirect DORA exposure via financial-entity customers; no direct ESA oversight.

## Composition

`dora + dora-non-ctpp`. Mutually exclusive with `dora-ctpp`.

## When to use

- ICT third-party providers serving EU financial entities at sub-CTPP scale
- Most SaaS vendors with EU financial-sector customers

## CTPP elevation risk

Providers approaching critical-mass EU financial-sector footprint should pre-emptively prepare for CTPP regime via `dora-ctpp` compliance posture — designation can occur with limited lead time.
