# REACH Tonnage Band ≥100 t/y — Open QMS chemicals class overlay

Chemicals class overlay encoding **REACH Annex IX** incremental data at ≥100 t/y per manufacturer/importer.

## Clauses

- Annex IX incremental (sub-chronic 90-day repeated dose; reproductive screening OECD TG 421/422 or EOGRTS; chronic Daphnia 21-day; long-term fish toxicity TG 210/215/234; soil + sediment ecotox; bird long-term where relevant; ultimate-degradation simulation in surface water + soil + sediment)
- Article 40 test-proposal mechanism — vertebrate studies MUST be proposed, not pre-conducted, with 45-day public consultation

## Composition

`chemicals + chemicals-tonnage-100`. Supersedes-and-includes chemicals-tonnage-1 + chemicals-tonnage-10.

## Adopter-cost note

The 100 t/y step adds the most expensive vertebrate study (90-day RDT typically €200-500k) and a much larger ecotox programme. Vertebrate test proposals require dossier-level planning + ECHA negotiation before any animal study commenced — multi-year timeline.
