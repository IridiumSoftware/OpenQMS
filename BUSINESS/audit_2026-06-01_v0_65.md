## Process note — why this audit reads differently

This release was produced through the GitHub PR/merge flow (8 PRs: #1 dashboard sync, #2 P15 schema, #3 P2 design, #4–#7 P2.1–P2.4 impl, #8 CI fix) rather than direct commits to `main`. The A6 finding surfaced *because* the merge step finally ran CI that had been dark. Recommendation carried forward: **enable branch protection on `main`** requiring the `engine-tests` check — the absence of it is precisely why a broken core gate went unnoticed for a week. (This is the adopter-side control that `openqms verify-deployment` / OQ-126 was built to check; the upstream project should eat its own dog food.)
